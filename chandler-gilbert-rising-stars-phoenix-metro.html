Chandler and Gilbert — The Rising Stars of the Phoenix Metro

By Sia Klaibua  ·  September 30, 2026  ·  6 min read  ·  NMLS# 2823225

Why the East Valley Is Winning Right Now

When people think of the Phoenix metro, Scottsdale and Paradise Valley come to mind first. But the buyers I am working with every week are increasingly pointing east — to Chandler and Gilbert — and the data backs them up completely. These two cities are not up-and-coming anymore. They have arrived, and smart buyers are taking notice before prices fully reflect that.

What $550,000 Buys You in Chandler Today

Chandler median sold price reached $550,000 in mid-2026, with average sold prices of $646,589 — up 1.6% year over year. Homes are taking approximately 49 days to sell, which gives today's buyers real time to be deliberate without losing opportunity. At that price point you are looking at a 3 to 4 bedroom home in an established neighborhood, often with good schools and reasonable HOA amenities. In Ocotillo or Fulton Ranch specifically, $550,000 to $700,000 puts you in resort-adjacent living that Scottsdale buyers would pay considerably more to access.

The employer base here matters too. Intel, PayPal, Amazon, and a growing semiconductor supply chain have created a workforce of high-income professionals who need housing close to their jobs. That demand is durable — it is not driven by speculation.

What $580,000 Buys You in Gilbert Today

Gilbert median sale price sits at approximately $580,000 as of mid-2026, with homes selling in around 45 days and receiving about 2 offers on average. Arizona Regional MLS projects 4% appreciation through the remainder of 2026, making Gilbert one of the stronger forecasted submarkets in the entire metro.

For that price you are getting more square footage than almost anywhere else in the Valley at a similar tier, in a city that consistently ranks among the safest in the country. The Heritage District has transformed Gilbert's identity — it is no longer a bedroom community. It is a destination.

The buyers who purchased in Chandler and Gilbert three years ago are sitting on equity that Scottsdale buyers at the same budget are still chasing.

What This Means If You Are Buying Now

With 30-year fixed rates at 7.54% as of September 30, 2026, and East Valley prices forecast to appreciate 3 to 7% through the rest of the year, the window for buying ahead of that appreciation is open — but not indefinitely. Inventory has improved, giving buyers negotiating room that did not exist two years ago. That combination of motivated sellers and rising fundamentals is rare, and it will not last.

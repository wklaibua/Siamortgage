A Step-by-Step Guide to Buying Your First Home in Scottsdale

By Sia Klaibua  ·  September 24, 2026  ·  7 min read  ·  NMLS# 2823225

Scottsdale in 2026 — More Accessible Than You Think

Scottsdale has a reputation for being out of reach for first-time buyers. And at the top of the market, that reputation is earned. But the full picture is more nuanced. There are Scottsdale condos and townhomes, entry-level homes in McCormick Ranch and South Scottsdale, and opportunities in the 85257 and 85250 zip codes that are genuinely within reach of a qualified first-time buyer in 2026.

Step 1 — Know Your Real Number Before You Fall in Love with a Home

At today's rate of 7.54%, your payment on a $500,000 purchase with 10% down is approximately $3,200 to $3,300 per month in principal and interest — before taxes, insurance, and HOA. That is the number you need to be comfortable with before you tour a single property. Fall in love with a payment, not a price tag.

Step 2 — Get Pre-Approved the Same Day You Decide to Look

I offer same-day pre-approval for qualified buyers. In Scottsdale specifically, even with more inventory in 2026, sellers in desirable neighborhoods are selective about which offers they respond to seriously. A pre-approval letter signals that you are a committed, qualified buyer — and it gives your agent the credibility to negotiate on your behalf.

Step 3 — Choose Your Agent and Your Lender at the Same Time

Your real estate agent and your mortgage lender are your two most important partners in this process. They need to communicate well and work on compatible timelines. In Scottsdale's market, a responsive lender who can confirm your qualification to a listing agent within hours of an offer makes a real difference in a competitive situation.

In Scottsdale, the buyers who win are not always the ones with the highest offer. They are the ones who show up the most prepared.

Steps 4 Through 7 — Search, Offer, Inspect, and Close

Once you are pre-approved and partnered with the right agent, define your non-negotiables: school district, commute distance, HOA preferences, pool, garage size. Scottsdale spans multiple distinct submarkets — from Old Town condos to North Scottsdale single-family homes — and knowing your priorities keeps your search focused. When you find the right home, move decisively. In 2026, approximately two out of three listings in the Phoenix metro are taking price reductions — which gives prepared buyers real negotiating leverage without sacrificing speed.

Once under contract, your home inspection and appraisal are standard steps I will guide you through clearly. Arizona closings typically take 21 to 30 days from contract to close.

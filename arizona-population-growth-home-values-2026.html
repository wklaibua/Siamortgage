Arizona Population Growth and What It Means for Home Values

By Sia Klaibua  ·  September 27, 2026  ·  5 min read  ·  NMLS# 2823225

Arizona Is Not Slowing Down

Every few months someone publishes a story about the Phoenix housing market cooling. And while it is true that the pace of appreciation has moderated from the extraordinary pace of 2021 and 2022, the underlying engine of this market has not changed. People are still moving here. Companies are still investing here. And housing demand is still running ahead of supply in most price ranges. The data as of 2026 makes this very clear.

Where the Growth Is Actually Happening

According to 2026 Arizona Census Bureau data, the biggest population gains are not happening in Phoenix proper, where growth has moderated to approximately 0.2%. The action is in the suburbs. Queen Creek led Arizona at 8.2% growth, followed by Goodyear at 6.5% and Apache Junction at 5.8%. The Phoenix metro as a whole has reached approximately 5.1 million residents with 1.5% overall population growth projected for 2026. These are not small numbers. This is sustained structural demand for housing.

The Job Growth That Matters

The Phoenix Metro added approximately 41,300 jobs in the 12 months ending July 2026, per the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Critically, the employment base is now genuinely diversified — spanning healthcare, logistics, finance, technology, and construction. This diversity makes Arizona significantly more resilient to sector-specific downturns than it was a decade ago, when the market was far more dependent on construction and real estate alone.

Population growth creates sustained housing demand. Arizona's fundamentals in 2026 — migration, job growth, and lifestyle appeal — continue to support home values across the metro.

What This Means for Your Purchase Decision

Home prices across the Phoenix metro are forecast to appreciate 3 to 5% through the remainder of 2026, with the strongest gains concentrated in the East Valley and the TSMC corridor in North Phoenix.

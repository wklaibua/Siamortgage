Is Now a Good Time to Buy a Home in Arizona?

By Sia Klaibua  ·  September 29, 2026  ·  5 min read  ·  NMLS# 2823225

The Honest Answer

It is the question I get every week without exception. And the honest answer is not a simple yes or no — it depends on your financial readiness, your timeline, and what you are comparing buying to. But here is what the data says as of September 30, 2026, and what I tell my clients based on what I see in this market every single day.

Where the Phoenix Market Actually Stands

The Phoenix metro median home price is hovering around $455,000, up approximately 1% year over year as of August 2026. Homes are spending an average of 57 to 60 days on the market — dramatically calmer than the 20-day average of peak 2022. Inventory has improved, and approximately two out of three listings in some price ranges are taking price reductions before closing. That means motivated sellers and real room to negotiate. That is genuinely unusual for this market, and it will not persist indefinitely.

What Rates Mean Right Now

As of September 30, 2026, the 30-year fixed rate is 7.54% per Mortgage News Daily — the highest since November 2023. One year ago that same rate was 6.38%. That matters to your payment, and I will not pretend otherwise. On a $455,000 purchase with 10% down, your principal and interest payment at 7.54% is approximately $2,870 per month.

But here is what most buyers overlook. Arizona home prices are forecast to appreciate 3 to 7% in most Phoenix metro submarkets through the remainder of 2026. On that same $455,000 home, a 5% increase adds $22,750 to the purchase price in one year. That is money no rate drop recovers. And when rates do fall, every buyer who has been waiting on the sidelines floods back in simultaneously — driving prices higher and putting bidding wars back on the table.

You can refinance a rate. You cannot go back and buy the same home at last year's price.

The Right Question to Ask

The right question is not whether rates are perfect. It is whether your finances are ready, your timeline makes sense, and whether the numbers work for your life right now. If those boxes are checked, waiting rarely produces the outcome buyers hope for in a market like Arizona's.

I would love to sit down with you — virtually or in person — and run the real numbers for your specific situation. One conversation is all it takes to know where you stand.
